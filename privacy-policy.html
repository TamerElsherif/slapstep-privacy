SlapStep Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Published by SlapStep Studios.

SlapStep is a game for children, so we keep privacy simple: we do not collect any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your child's progress (stars, the last level played, a run in progress), settings (sound, language, digits, easy math) and any redeemed codes are saved only on this device and are excluded from cloud backups.
  • We cannot see this information. It never leaves the device. If you delete the app or press "Reset progress" in the Parents area, it is erased.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not ask for your child's name, age, location, photos, contacts or microphone.
  • We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Codes

Parents can type a tester or redeem code in the Parents area. The game checks the code on the device, without going online, and remembers the result on the device. Codes contain no personal information.

Purchases

The game has one optional purchase that unlocks all worlds. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the app only whether the purchase was made, and the app remembers that on the device. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase. Purchase screens are placed behind a grown-ups-only check.

Google Play

Google Play may collect technical information about the app, such as crash reports, under Google's own privacy policy. We only see anonymous, combined statistics from it.

Children

The game is made for children aged 4 to 9. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here and update the effective date.
